# Privacy

A21 Game Optimizer is local and offline.

- It declares no internet permission.
- It collects no analytics, identifiers, account information, passwords, or tokens.
- It does not read or modify Call of Duty: Mobile files.
- It does not use Accessibility, VPN, overlay, root, usage-access, or foreground-service permissions.
- The only persistent values are the selected performance profile and selected official regional package, stored locally in Android SharedPreferences.
- Device RAM, sticky battery state, power-saver state, and the public Android thermal status are read on-device and are never transmitted.

Uninstalling the app removes its local preferences. Android backup is disabled for this app.
